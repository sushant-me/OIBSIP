01 — About

Curious by default, careful by design.

I am a web developer and security researcher from Kathmandu who cares about the quiet details: semantic markup, layouts that hold up on a 360px screen, and interfaces that explain themselves without a manual. I like building small, complete things — a landing page, a converter, a portfolio — and getting every state of them right.

My security work grew out of that same curiosity. I study the OWASP Top 10, practise responsible disclosure in labs, and try to think like the person on the other side of the form field. The best interface is one that is both pleasant to use and boring to attack.

Right now I am completing the Oasis Infobyte Web Development & Designing internship, where these three Level 1 projects are the foundation of a larger public portfolio.
