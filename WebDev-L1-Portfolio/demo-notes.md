# Demo notes — personal portfolio

1. Open <http://localhost:8000>. The sticky header shows the SP monogram, name, four nav links and the "Let's talk" button.
2. Check the hero: name, role line, intro, two buttons, the avatar placeholder with the "Available" badge, and the stack strip underneath.
3. Click "About". The page scrolls smoothly to the About section and the About link in the header becomes highlighted.
4. Click "Skills". Six cards are shown in two rows on desktop; resize to 360px and they stack in one column.
5. Click "Projects". Three project cards are visible. Hover a card to see it lift and the arrow move.
6. Click any project card. It opens `https://github.com/sushant-me` in a new tab (target="_blank" with rel="noopener noreferrer").
7. Click "Contact". The email button opens a mail client for `sushant.poudel2028@gmail.com`.
8. In the contact block, hover the GitHub, LinkedIn and email icons. Each is labelled for screen readers; LinkedIn is a placeholder link.
9. Press Tab from the top of the page. The skip link appears first, then nav links, buttons and cards all show a visible teal focus ring.
10. Resize the browser to 768px and 360px. The header wraps to two rows, cards stack, and there is no horizontal scrolling.
11. Scroll to the footer. The year is filled in by JavaScript; with JS disabled it falls back to 2026.
