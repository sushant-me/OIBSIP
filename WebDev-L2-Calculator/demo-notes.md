# Demo notes — Calculator

Use these steps to show every feature in a short walkthrough.

1. Open the page. Point out the two-line display: `Ready` above, `0` below, and the
   four-column keypad.
2. Click `5`, `+`, `3`, `×`, `2`. Read the expression line aloud: `5 + 3 × 2`.
3. Press `=`. The display shows `11` and the right-hand History panel records
   `5 + 3 × 2 = 11`.
4. Press `+`, `4`, `=` to show chaining from the result (`15`).
5. Press `C`, then `7`, `÷`, `0`, `=`. Point out the friendly "Cannot divide by zero"
   message; there is no `Infinity` anywhere.
6. Type a digit (say `9`) to show the calculator recovers from the error.
7. Press `C`, then type `1`, `2`, `3`. Press the backspace key twice and watch `123 → 12 → 1`.
8. Press `C` and try `1`, `.`, `2`, `.`, `3` — the second decimal is ignored, so the
   display stays `1.23`.
9. Press `C`, then `5`, `+`, `3`, `=`, `=`, `=`. The results step `8 → 11 → 14` to show
   repeated equals.
10. Press `C`, then `200`, `×`, `5`, `%`, `=`. The answer is `10`, because `%` turns the
    current entry into `0.05`.
11. Press `C`, then `5`, `±` to show `-5`, and `±` again to return to `5`.
12. Do the same flows from the keyboard: type `9 * 9 Enter`, then `Esc` to clear.
13. Scroll the History panel, click an older row, and show its result loading back into
    the display. Press `+ 10 =` to carry on from it.
14. Press Clear history; the panel returns to its empty message.
15. Resize the window to phone width to show the single-column layout with the keypad
    still on four columns.
