A password we never see.

Registration checks your password rules as you type, then stores only a random salt and a SHA-256 hash. Signing in hashes your input again and compares the result.

  • Two password rules, checked live At least 8 characters and at least 1 number — both tick green while you type.
  • Usernames and emails stay unique If either one is already registered, the form says so and keeps your input.
  • What actually gets stored Username, email, a 32-character salt and a 64-character hash. That is all.
