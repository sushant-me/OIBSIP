Latch

Front-end authentication demo

Your account, locked to a hash.

A complete registration, sign-in and session flow that runs entirely in the browser. Passwords are salted and hashed with SHA-256 — never stored in plaintext.

  • Salted SHA-256 Hashed in the browser with crypto.subtle.digest; only the salt and hash are saved.
  • Session-guarded dashboard No session? dashboard.html redirects you straight back to sign in.
  • Nothing leaves the browser Accounts live in localStorage, the session in sessionStorage. No backend, no network calls.
