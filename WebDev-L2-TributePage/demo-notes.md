# Demo notes — Tribute Page

A short scroll-through for recording; nothing on the page needs clicking.

1. Start at the top. Read the title `Ada Lovelace` and the one-line tagline underneath it.
2. Point at the portrait and read the caption: Chalon, c. 1840, Science Museum Group,
   public domain, via Wikimedia Commons.
3. Scroll to the biography section. Mention the drop cap and that the four paragraphs are
   original writing; pause on the sentences about Babbage and the Analytical Engine.
4. Scroll into the green "A life in dates" section. Read two or three entries — 1815,
   1843 and 1980 are the useful ones.
5. Continue to the red quote band. Read the Note G passage and its citation, then the note
   about Turing below it.
6. Scroll to "A name on a language" and the "In her honour" card. Mention MIL-STD-1815
   being numbered after her birth year.
7. Finish at the footer, which repeats the portrait credit and the author line.
8. Resize the window to a phone width to show the hero stacking and the timeline switching
   to a single column with the year above each entry. Press "Back to top".
