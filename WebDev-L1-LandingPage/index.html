Features

Built for the way focus actually works.

Most tools ask you to manage them. Aether asks one question — what matters today — and then gets out of the way until the session ends.

One-click focus blocks

Choose 25, 50 or 90 minutes and start. Aether dims everything else and ends the block with a soft chime — not a notification pile.

Task triage that respects you

Each morning Aether asks for the three things that would make today matter. Everything else waits quietly in the backlog.

Session notes, auto-tied

Jot a line the moment you stop. Notes attach to both the session and the task, so context survives the end of the day.

Quiet analytics

Weekly truth instead of vanity charts: deep-work hours, interruptions, and the tasks you actually finished — delivered in one calm email.

Offline by default

Every session is written locally first and syncs when you are ready. Your focus data never depends on a connection.

Your team, without the surveillance

Share availability, not keystrokes. Teammates see when you are heads-down and when you are free — never what is on your screen.

How it works

Three steps. Then silence.

No onboarding maze, no setup ritual. Aether is designed so the product disappears while the work happens.

  1. 01

    Pick three

    Open Aether and write the three outcomes that would make today count. Aether keeps them pinned until you close them or deliberately drop them.

  2. 02

    Start the block

    Pick a length and press start. The screen fades, the timer becomes the only moving thing, and the session is captured automatically.

  3. 03

    Close the loop

    End with one line of reflection. Tomorrow begins with context instead of guesswork — and the week ends with an honest summary.

The whole idea in one line: pick three things, start a timer, write one line at the end. Everything else in Aether is optional.
